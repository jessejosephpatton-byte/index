The Profile
Whiskey-forward and unapologetically spirit-led. Drawn to bold, assertive modifiers — amaro, Campari, Fernet, Bénédictine — that announce themselves rather than whisper. Bitters are essential, not optional. Sweetness is welcome only as counterbalance, never as the point. Smoke is consistently compelling: Laphroaig, mezcal, peated Scotch. Stirred and spirit-forward is the default mode; shaken only when the build demands it.

The Penicillin Lesson
Even sour-format drinks work when smoke and bitterness lead, and citrus/honey play a structural rather than starring role. The Laphroaig float means the first thing that hits is peat and smoke — the lemon and honey are the backbone, not the headline.
