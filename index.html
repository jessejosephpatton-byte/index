The Profile
Whiskey-forward and unapologetically spirit-led. Drawn to bold, assertive modifiers — amaro, Campari, Fernet, Bénédictine — that announce themselves rather than whisper. Bitters are essential, not optional. Sweetness is welcome only as counterbalance, never as the point. Smoke is consistently compelling: Laphroaig, mezcal, peated Scotch. The Penicillin insight: even sour-format drinks work when smoke and bitterness lead, and citrus/honey play structural rather than starring roles.
